# Pizza Dough

## 4-7 people
  - 450-500ml water (lower in summer)
  - 775g all-purpose flour
  - 20g vital wheat gluten
  - 10g honey
  - 5g yeast
  - 20g salt

## 2-3 people
  - 225-250ml water (lower in summer)
  - 387g all-purpose flour
  - 10g vital wheat gluten
  - 5g honey
  - 3g yeast
  - 10g salt
