# Spaghetti

## Sauce
 - 2 28oz cans crushed tomatos
 - 2 6oz cans tomato paste
 - 2 cloves garlic
 - 1-2 tbsp basil
 - 2 tbsp olive oil
 - 1/2 cup sugar
 - 1/4 cup onion (optional)
 - 1/2 cup fresh tomato (optional)

Sweat garlic (and onion, optionally) in olive oil. Add all ingredients
except tomato. Optionally stir in tomato just before serving.

## Meatballs
 - 1lb ground beef
 - 1lb hot italian sausage
 - 1/2 cup bread crumbs
 - 1/4 cup milk
 - 1/2 cup parmesan or asiago cheese
 - 1 egg
 - 2 tsp italian herb
 - 2 tsp olive oil
 - 1/2 tsp garlic powder
 - 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
 - 1 1/2 tsp salt
 - 1/2 tsp ground black pepper

Mix all raw ingredients together. Using a melon baller, scoop into
3/4"-1"balls Bake at 350F until browned through.
